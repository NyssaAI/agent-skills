# Calibrate from evidence

## Gather the useful inputs

Use supplied or explicitly selected samples and stated preferences. Identify authorship and context: user-authored material, AI-authored drafts, quoted text, and house requirements carry different evidence. Do not use every sentence in a mixed document as evidence of the user's voice. Ask for additional inputs only when they materially affect the requested maintenance; sparse evidence can still support a limited profile.

Extract a few observable patterns: sentence length and variation, contractions, vocabulary, transitions, directness, warmth, paragraph shape, opening and closing habits. Distinguish a repeated choice from a topic-specific coincidence. Do not infer beliefs, commitments, personality diagnoses, or universal preferences from subject matter.

## Propose and compare

Present inferred patterns with supporting sample labels, scope, confidence, and contradictions. Favor concrete rules such as “ask for the action in the opening sentence of routine emails” over labels such as “authentic” or “bold.” Mark single-sample patterns as tentative. The user's explicit preference governs when it contradicts a sample.

When useful, create one or two short calibration drafts in the requested context using identical supplied facts. Keep comparisons in the scratch location from [storage](storage.md). Explain the meaningful style difference and use the user's preference judgments to choose. Do not require an elaborate onboarding exercise for a simple requested preference update.

If approved examples are available, compare against a relevant example not used to derive the candidate pattern. Check factual preservation separately from resemblance. The user's judgment and held-out evidence are stronger than an agent's self-score; do not claim proven voice accuracy from self-assessment or packaging checks.

## Accept within scope

Persist explicit requested preferences and already accepted rules without redundant confirmation. Present uncertain inferred changes for approval before saving them. Approval of a draft alone is not permission to retain its text as an example or to update a profile. Keep unresolved hypotheses in the conversation or disposable comparison material, outside accepted profile guidance. Use [learning from edits](learning-from-edits.md) when the user authorizes learning from corrections.
