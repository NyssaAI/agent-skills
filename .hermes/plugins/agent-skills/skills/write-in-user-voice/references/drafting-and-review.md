# Drafting and review

## Apply the right guidance

Use the profile for the active project, resolved through [storage](../../maintain-writing-voice/references/storage.md). Follow current explicit directions and required format or destination standards first. Apply approved explicit preferences and the relevant context mode, then supported inferred habits. Read relevant examples on demand rather than copying every example into the task.

An email may need an opening, context, and a clear request; chat may convey the same facts in one paragraph. Choose the mode supported by the profile and task. Avoid caricature: repeating a signature phrase or forcing every sentence to match one sample does not establish resemblance.

House-style-governed output uses the required house style. Apply compatible personal habits only where appropriate. Do not rewrite quoted passages, native email/calendar evidence, or third-party material into the user's voice unless the task specifically calls for a transformed version; preserve source identity and attribution.

If no profile is present, complete useful drafting with the explicit instructions and any samples authorized for this task. Briefly explain that a saved profile was unavailable and offer setup without requiring it. Do not create profile directories, save inferred rules, or claim the draft matches established preferences.

## Preserve meaning

Before polishing, identify the factual constraints: names, dates, amounts, events, causal claims, evidence, requested actions, commitments, intent, and degree of certainty. Compare the draft against them afterward. Do not add unsupported enthusiasm, agreement, promises, beliefs, facts, or intimacy merely to sound like the user. Preserve uncertainty such as “may” versus “will.” If a needed fact is missing, use an explicit placeholder or ask for it when necessary; never invent it.

For a rewrite, retain substantive content unless the user authorized changing it. Compression may remove repetition but must not erase a qualification or change the requested action. Resolve incompatible constraints honestly; voice guidance cannot override the task's factual meaning.

## Final check

- Meaning: are facts, commitments, intent, and uncertainty preserved?
- Voice: does the draft follow the applicable mode, explicit preferences, and supported patterns without exaggeration?
- Destination: does it meet the requested audience, format, evidence, and editorial requirements?
- Evidence: does comparison with an approved relevant example support the claimed resemblance? Qualify absent or sparse evidence.
- Boundary: did profile files remain unchanged, with no automatic learning, sample retention, or sending/publishing?

Deliver the text with only the explanation useful to the user. Requested draft corrections stay task-local unless the user authorizes [profile maintenance](../../maintain-writing-voice/SKILL.md).
